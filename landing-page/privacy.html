Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-11

Turbolt does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing data to any server operated by the Turbolt developers — because no such server exists. The entire extension runs locally in your browser.

Permissions requested and why

PermissionWhy it's needed
downloadsSave downloaded files to your device via the browser's standard downloads API.
storageStore the download queue and settings locally on your device (not synced anywhere by Turbolt itself).
notificationsShow a notification when a download finishes.
webRequestObserve request URLs to detect .m3u8/.mpd video manifests — request bodies and other traffic are not read or stored.
contextMenusAdd a "Download with Turbolt" item to the right-click menu.
host_permissions: <all_urls>Allow downloading files and detecting video streams on any site you choose to download from — Turbolt only acts when you initiate a download or view its popup, and never runs a background scrape of page content.

What Turbolt does NOT do

  • No ads, no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No transmission of browsing history, tab lists, or page content anywhere.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties — there is no data collected to begin with.
  • No account or sign-in required.

Open source = verifiable

Turbolt is open source under the GPL-3.0 license. Anyone can read the full source code on GitHub to independently verify every claim on this page.
