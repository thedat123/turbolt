Frequently asked questions

Is Turbolt really free, unlike IDM?

Yes. Turbolt has no trial period, license key, or premium tier — it's licensed under GPL-3.0, which legally requires it (and any fork) to stay free and open source.

Can Turbolt download HLS (.m3u8) videos?

Yes. Turbolt watches for HLS and DASH manifests as you browse and lets you download the full stream as a single file. It intentionally does not support DRM-protected content (e.g. Netflix, YouTube Premium).

Does Turbolt collect any data?

No. Turbolt has no analytics, no tracking, and no account system. Everything runs locally in your browser, and the full source is public so anyone can verify this.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers via Manifest V3. Firefox support is on the roadmap.

Why are some downloads not multi-threaded?

Some servers don't support HTTP Range requests, or a file may exceed the configured size limit for in-memory multi-threading. Turbolt automatically falls back to your browser's native downloader in these cases — downloads never fail silently.
